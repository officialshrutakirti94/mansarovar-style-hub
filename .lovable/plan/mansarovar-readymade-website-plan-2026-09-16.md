# Mansarovar Readymade website plan

## Build
- Replace the blank page with a responsive one-page fashion storefront.
- Lead with the real shop photograph, prominently layer the Mansarovar logo, headline, supporting copy, and one “Explore Collections” action.
- Add Men’s, Ladies’, and Kids’ collection cards with cohesive fashion imagery, including Boys and Girls labels under Kids’ Wear.
- Add a concise store introduction, contact area with placeholder phone/email, and the supplied Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook links.
- Finish with a compact footer containing the logo, navigation, social links, and contact details.

## Visual direction
- Use the logo’s red, orange, yellow, and warm neutral palette with premium editorial typography.
- Add restrained glass effects, fine line details, gentle floating fashion motifs, smooth scrolling, and subtle interaction motion.
- Keep the shop identity and real storefront visible immediately on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

## Assets and details
- Store the uploaded shop photo and logo through the project asset system; create a square favicon from the logo.
- Generate three coordinated fashion-category images that feel polished and locally relevant.
- Add accessible labels, meaningful image descriptions, and page-specific search/social metadata.

## Verification
- Check the finished page at desktop and mobile sizes, confirm all links and the collection scroll action work, and resolve any visual or loading errors.
